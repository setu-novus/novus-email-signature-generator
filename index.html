Outlook setup instructions

Follow the instructions for your version of Outlook. You can create a new signature or replace your existing one.

Adding a new signature

  1. Complete your details above and click Copy signature.
  2. Open New Outlook or Outlook on the web.
  3. Click the Settings gear icon.
  4. Navigate to Accounts > Signatures.
  5. Click Add signature or New signature.
  6. Give the signature a name, for example SETU Novus.
  7. Click inside the signature editor and press Ctrl + V to paste.
  8. Choose the signature for new messages and replies/forwards if required.
  9. Click Save.

Replacing an existing signature

  1. Complete your details above and click Copy signature.
  2. Open Outlook and click the Settings gear icon.
  3. Go to Accounts > Signatures.
  4. Select your existing signature from the list.
  5. Click inside its editing area and press Ctrl + A to select the old content.
  6. Press Ctrl + V to replace it with your new SETU Novus signature.
  7. Check that the logo, text and links appear correctly.
  8. Confirm your default signature settings and click Save.
Tip: If the signature formatting changes when pasted into desktop Outlook, try using Outlook on the web. HTML signatures often paste more reliably there.

Adding a new signature

  1. Complete your details and click Copy signature.
  2. Open Classic Outlook for Windows.
  3. Go to File > Options > Mail > Signatures.
  4. Click New.
  5. Enter a name such as SETU Novus.
  6. Click inside the signature editing area.
  7. Press Ctrl + V to paste.
  8. Under Choose default signature, select the correct email account.
  9. Set the signature for new messages and replies/forwards as required.
  10. Click OK to save.

Replacing an existing signature

  1. Complete your details and click Copy signature.
  2. Open Classic Outlook.
  3. Go to File > Options > Mail > Signatures.
  4. Under Select signature to edit, click your current signature.
  5. Click inside the signature editing area.
  6. Press Ctrl + A to select the old signature.
  7. Press Ctrl + V to replace it with the new signature.
  8. Check the logo and formatting.
  9. Ensure the correct default signature is selected.
  10. Click OK to save changes.
Important: When replacing a signature, select the existing signature first. Do not create another signature unless you want to keep both versions.

Adding a new signature

  1. Complete your details and click Copy signature.
  2. Open Outlook for Mac.
  3. Go to Outlook > Settings > Signatures.
  4. Click the + or Add button.
  5. Name your signature SETU Novus.
  6. Click inside the signature editor and press Cmd + V.
  7. Save your signature.
  8. Set it as your default signature if required.

Replacing an existing signature

  1. Complete your details and click Copy signature.
  2. Open Outlook for Mac.
  3. Go to Outlook > Settings > Signatures.
  4. Select the signature you currently use.
  5. Open it for editing if required.
  6. Click inside the editing area and press Cmd + A.
  7. Press Cmd + V to paste your replacement signature.
  8. Check the logo and formatting.
  9. Save the changes and confirm your default signature settings.
Tip: The appearance of the signature settings may vary depending on your version of Outlook for Mac.
